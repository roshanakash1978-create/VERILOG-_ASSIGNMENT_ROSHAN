The 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder is a circuit. It is made up of four Full Adders that are connected together. This project has all the details about the hardware. It also has a testbench and simulation waveforms to show how the 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder works.

## Design Overview

The 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder is a circuit that adds two binary numbers together. It is made by connecting 1-bit full adders one, after the other. The carry output of each adder is connected to the carry input of the next full adder. This is how the 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder does its job. The 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder is a way to add binary numbers. The Full Adders are the building blocks of the 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder.
